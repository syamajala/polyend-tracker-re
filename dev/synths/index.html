    Synths¶

    Every synth model, how it works and what each setting does. The Settings part of each page is also what the help box in Edit Patch shows.

    Synth
    8-Type A chip-style synth in the spirit of the 3-voice sound chips of 8-bit home computers: three oscillators with combined waves, ring modulation and sync, one multimode filter, a stepped envelope and the chip tricks.
    A-Type A two-oscillator synth that can play two notes at once, after the semi-modular duophonic synths of the 1970s: sync, ring modulation, sample & hold and two envelopes.
    ACD A one-oscillator acid synth after the Tracker Mini's ACD: saw, square, sub and noise into a resonant low-pass, with a modulation envelope, one LFO and glide.
    Arc-Type A chip-style FM voice in the spirit of the 4-operator FM sound chips of home consoles, with their square and noise companion: four sine operators, envelopes counted in chip rates, a 9-bit output and the chip arpeggio.
    B-Type A buzzy 8-bit chip voice: square waves, noisy buzz shapes, stepped volume and the fast arpeggios of old home computer music.
    CS-Type A polysynth with two layers per note, after the large dual-channel polysynths of the late 1970s: two full synth voices stacked, a ring modulator, and touch control.
    D-Type A three-oscillator synth with a ladder filter, after the classic monophonic synths of the early 1970s.
    DX7 A six-operator FM engine that plays Yamaha DX7 voices (.syx files). Loading voices, banks and playing are on the DX7 page.
    FAT A unison saw synth after the Tracker Mini's FAT: a stack of up to seven detuned saws into a low-pass filter, with one LFO and glide.
    FM4 A four-operator FM synth in the spirit of the classic 4-op FM chips: four sine operators in eight algorithms, feedback on operator 1, and an envelope per operator.
    Hand-Type A chip-style synth in the spirit of the 4-channel sound chip of a handheld console: one patch is one channel (Pulse, Wave or Noise) or a whole drum kit, with a stepped envelope and the chip tricks.
    JU6 A polysynth with one oscillator per voice, a resonant filter, one envelope and a built-in stereo chorus, after the one-oscillator polysynths of the early 1980s.
    PERC A drum machine after the Tracker Mini's PERC: one patch is a kit of six drums, each with its own mixer channel, and a waveshaper and EQ on the kit.
    T-Type A drum synth after the classic transistor drum machines: one patch is a kit of ten drum channels, every drum built from sines with pitch sweeps, filtered noise, struck resonators and a bank of six square oscillators.
    VAP A two-oscillator virtual analog polysynth after the Tracker Mini's VAP: oscillators that morph from triangle to saw to pulse, a 15-type filter, three envelopes, two LFOs and a modulation matrix.
    WTFM A two-operator FM synth with shapeable operators, after the Tracker Mini's WTFM: each operator plays a waveform row you can morph, operator 2 modulates operator 1, and a filter, three envelopes, two LFOs and a modulation matrix follow.
